# paweh 0.99.0

* Initial Bioconductor submission candidate.
* Provides prospective power and minimum sample size calculations for
  case-control, TDT/family-based, quantitative-trait, sequencing-based, and
  LRTae study designs.
* Supports selected forms of locus heterogeneity and genotype and phenotype
  misclassification.
* Includes static visualization functions and an optional interactive Shiny
  dashboard.
