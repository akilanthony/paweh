Provides analytic power and minimum sample size necessary (MSSN)
calculations for genetic association study designs described in Gordon
et al. (2020), "Heterogeneity in Statistical Genetics". Public
interfaces cover case-control genotype chi-square and trend tests,
family-based transmission disequilibrium tests (TDT), and single- and
multivariate quantitative-trait designs using ANOVA, threshold-selected
genotype chi-square tests, and Pillai MANOVA. Methods can account for
locus heterogeneity and supported phenotype or genotype
misclassification mechanisms. Functions return structured results and
include static and interactive visualizations for study design,
teaching, and methodological validation.
